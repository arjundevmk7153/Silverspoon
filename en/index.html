About the chef

Silver Spoon is a small Berlin home bakery run by chef Neethu, who bakes and decorates every single cake herself — from the first sketch of your theme to the final sugar flower. Nothing sits on a shelf: every cake, brownie and cupcake is baked fresh only when you order it.

She holds a Master's degree in Computer Science — but chose the oven over the keyboard, training as a chef through professional baking courses before founding Silver Spoon. Her baking brings together classic patisserie and the flavours she grew up with in Kerala, India — which is why you'll find ghee cake and Kerala-style tea cake right next to white chocolate and berry cakes on the menu.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I order?

Every cake is baked fresh on order, so a few days' notice is ideal for simple cakes. For themed decorations and double-tier cakes, one to two weeks gives us time to make it perfect. Short on time? Message us anyway — we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

What do the cakes cost?

Prices are transparent: simple birthday cakes start at €90, big double-tier customised cakes go up to €200, and everything else sits in between. Brownies are €3 per piece. You'll always know the price before we start baking.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is available only for double-tier cakes. All other cakes are pickup only — but we're sure you won't regret coming: the moment you take the first bite, you'll know it was worth the trip.

Can you make my theme?

Yes! The designs you see on this page are just examples — whatever theme you have in mind, we will make it, on any flavour you prefer. When ordering, just send a sample picture of the cake you'd like or describe your idea.

Do you have vegan options?

Not all our cakes are vegan, so please specify when ordering if you need a vegan cake and we'll bake yours accordingly.

Can I order brownies in bulk?

Absolutely — brownies are €3 per piece, and for bulk orders (parties, offices, events) the price can be discussed over phone. Just give us a call or message us on WhatsApp.
